About Fontbend

Fontbend is a font maker that runs in your browser. You shape an open-source font with sliders and download it as a font of your own.

Made by Enes Kır eneskir.com

Licenses

All base fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. You can use, change and share them, including in commercial work. Keep their copyright lines, and don't sell a font file on its own.

A font you make here keeps the license of the font it came from. Fontbend adds “Made with Fontbend” to its description.

  • HarfBuzzMIT
  • woff2MIT
  • Base fontsOFL 1.1

Privacy

Font files you open stay on your computer and are never uploaded. There are no accounts and no tracking.

Recent fonts are kept in this browser's storage. Clearing the site's data removes them.
