Fontbend makes WOFF2 files with woff2-encoder 2.0.0 (https://github.com/itskyedo/woff2-encoder),
which compiles Google's WOFF2 library (https://github.com/google/woff2, commit 4721483)
and Brotli 1.0.3 (https://github.com/google/brotli) to WebAssembly.
